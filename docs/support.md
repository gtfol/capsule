# capsule scan support

Contact [team@gtfol.dev](mailto:team@gtfol.dev) for help with capsule scan. Include your app version, iOS version, and what happened. Never send passwords, integration tokens, or API keys.

## photo details

Without an OpenAI key, capsule scan estimates color on your iPhone. Name, brand, and category remain editable and are not filled in automatically.

For optional name, brand, and category suggestions, open Settings → photo details, enter your own OpenAI API key, and allow photo processing. New garment photos are then sent directly to OpenAI. API usage is billed separately by OpenAI. You can remove your key in Settings at any time.

If photo processing fails, you can still edit and save the item. This version keeps the photo's background; it does not cut out the garment.

## saving to capsule

Sign in to your capsule account before capturing an item. Review the fields and tap **save to capsule**. Unsaved or interrupted scans can be kept in drafts on your iPhone. If a save fails, open the draft and retry when connected.

## your account

Manage your wardrobe or delete your capsule account at [capsule settings](https://capsule.gtfol.dev/?view=settings). You may need to sign in in your browser. Account deletion removes cloud account data; local drafts remain on this iPhone until the app is deleted.

See the [privacy policy](https://capsule.gtfol.dev/privacy) for details about storage and photo processing.
